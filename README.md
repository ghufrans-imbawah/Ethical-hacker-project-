# Ethical-hacker-project-
An academic cybersecurity project exploring ethical hacking, its tools, and its role in protecting digital systems.
The Ethical Hacker

Project Overview

The Ethical Hacker is an academic cybersecurity project completed as part of the Introduction to Security course at Umm Al-Qura University. The project explores the role of ethical hackers, common hacking tools, and the importance of ethical hacking in protecting organizations and digital systems from cyber threats.

My Contribution

My main responsibility was designing the visual materials for the project booth presentation. I created digital slides and posters to explain the concept of ethical hacking, showcase examples of hacking tools, and demonstrate how ethical hacking helps organizations improve their security.

Key Learning Outcomes

* Developed skills in visual communication and presentation design.
* Learned how to organize technical information in a clear and engaging way.
* Improved creativity and attention to visual details.
* Gained a better understanding of ethical hacking and its role in cybersecurity.
